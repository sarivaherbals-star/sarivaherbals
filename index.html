About us

Where Ayurvedic knowledge meets formulation science

Sariva Herbals (OPC) Private Limited is a Karnataka-based start-up founded by a pharmaceutics academic to take well-researched herbal formulations from the laboratory to the people who need them. Our name comes from Sariva (Indian sarsaparilla, Hemidesmus indicus) — a classical Ayurvedic herb and one of the ingredients in our own lead formula.

We follow a simple rule: every product begins with published scientific evidence for its botanicals, is developed using standardised extracts and modern dosage-form design, and is evaluated for safety and performance before it reaches the market.

Our technology

The DR-32® botanical platform

At the heart of our work is a proprietary spagyric distillate prepared from a large combination of medicinal plants using a traditional cohobation process — repeated distillation that returns the distillate to the plant material to concentrate its active constituents.

We are characterising the distillate chemically using high-resolution LC-MS and combining it with standardised herbal extracts to build a family of finished products. DR-32 is a registered trademark of the company; the exact composition is held as proprietary know-how.

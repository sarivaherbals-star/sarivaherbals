About us

Where traditional wisdom meets formulation science

Sariva Herbals (OPC) Private Limited is a woman-led, Karnataka-based herbal innovation company. We take well-researched botanical actives from the laboratory to the people who need them — through careful formulation, modern delivery technologies and rigorous testing.

Our guiding principle is simple: every product begins with scientific evidence, is built with standardised ingredients and modern dosage-form design, and is evaluated for safety and performance before it reaches the market.

MissionSafe, affordable and effective herbal health products, backed by data.
VisionA trusted Indian name in science-validated botanical healthcare.
ValuesEvidence first, quality always, respect for tradition.

Our technology

Platforms that make herbal actives work harder

Many plant actives are powerful but poorly absorbed. Our platforms solve that delivery problem.

Nanovesicular delivery

Ultra-deformable nanovesicles that carry herbal actives through the skin barrier into the target layers, forming a local depot for sustained action — incorporated into patient-friendly gels.

DR-32® polyherbal platform

Our proprietary multi-botanical base formula, developed in-house and held as protected know-how, serving as the foundation for a family of finished products.

Quality by design

Standardised extracts, modern analytical characterisation and stability testing to ensure every batch is consistent, safe and effective.
